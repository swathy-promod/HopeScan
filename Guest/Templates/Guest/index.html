Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about booking, services, and patient care at BMH Hospital.

How can I book an appointment at BMH Hospital?

You can book an appointment online using the appointment form, by calling +91 6282121953, or by visiting the hospital reception. Our staff will confirm your preferred date and time.

What documents are needed for first-time registration?

Please bring a valid government-issued ID, proof of address, insurance information (if applicable), and any previous medical reports to help our team understand your health history.

Does BMH Hospital accept health insurance?

Yes, we accept most major health insurance plans. Contact our billing office before your visit to verify coverage, confirm cashless admission options, and understand any co-pay requirements.

What are the visiting hours for admitted patients?

Visiting hours are generally from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day. Please note that ICU, emergency, and surgical ward visiting hours may vary, so check with the nursing staff first.

Can I consult a specialist online or via telemedicine?

Teleconsultation services are available for follow-up appointments and selected specialties. Please contact us through the website or phone to schedule a virtual consultation.

How can I get emergency care at BMH Hospital?

Our emergency department is open 24/7. For urgent medical needs, come directly to the hospital or call our emergency number so our team can prepare for your arrival.
